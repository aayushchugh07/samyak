About Samyak Computer Classes

Samyak Computer Classes is a computer training institute in Indore that teaches both technical and non-technical skills. Whether you are a school student, a college student, a fresher or a working professional, our courses are designed to be practical, easy to follow and useful in a real job.

Every course combines classroom teaching, lab practice and projects. We also help learners with resume building, interview preparation and internship opportunities, so they are ready when hiring opportunities come up.

Our mission

Make quality computer education simple, affordable and job-focused for everyone in Indore.

Our vision

To be the most trusted skill-training institute for students and professionals in the city.

Courses that build real skills

Choose from technical and non-technical programs. Every course includes practice sessions and projects.

Course duration, syllabus and fees may vary by batch and branch. Please confirm the latest details with your nearest branch before enrolling.

Internships

Work on real projects, learn team workflows and add practical experience to your resume.

Internship availability depends on the current batch and project openings. An internship is a learning opportunity and does not guarantee a job.
